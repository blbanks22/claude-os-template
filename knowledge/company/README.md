# knowledge/company/

Research notes on specific companies: employers, prospects, competitors, or companies whose people you will interview with.

Suggested file pattern: `<company-slug>.md`, e.g., `stripe.md`, `anthropic.md`.

Suggested contents per company file:

- One-paragraph "what they do" in plain English.
- Stage, headcount, funding (with date).
- Recent strategic moves and what they signal.
- Their stated and unstated thesis.
- Key people and links (linking to `knowledge/people/<name>.md`).
- Sources with dates accessed.

The `interview-prep` and `competitive-teardown` skills will read from here when context exists.
