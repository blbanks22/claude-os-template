# knowledge/notes/

Free-form notes that do not fit `career/`, `company/`, `people/`, or `reading/`.

Common subdirectories that skills create here:

- `postmortems/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<project-slug>.md` — written by the `product-postmortem` skill.
- `weekly-reviews/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md` — written by the `weekly-review` skill.

You may also add your own ad-hoc notes here: decision memos, working drafts, half-formed ideas, conference takeaways that do not warrant a full `reading/` synthesis.

If a topic accumulates more than ~5 files, consider moving it into its own subdirectory with an `INDEX.md`.
