# knowledge/people/

Notes on people in your network or who you will meet.

Suggested file pattern: `<first-last>.md`.

Suggested contents per person:

- One sentence on who they are and how you know them (or how you might meet).
- Public links: LinkedIn, blog, Twitter, recent talks.
- What they care about, based on their public output.
- Any prior conversation summary or context.
- For interviewers: their tenure at the company, their team, what they are likely to ask.

The `interview-prep` skill will read here when you give it an interviewer name.

## Privacy

Do not commit private contact information (phone, personal email) here. Public profile links only.
